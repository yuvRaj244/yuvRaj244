## Featured project

**Credit card cashback A/B test: the offer loses money**

A simulated A/B test of a credit card cashback offer, built in SQL and Python. The headline finding is that around 95% of the reward budget goes to customers who would have spent anyway, so the incremental revenue never covers the payout. The interesting part is not that the treatment group spent more, it is that almost all of that spend was not caused by the offer.

What it shows: experiment design, sample ratio mismatch check, A/A test, power analysis, and the financial reasoning that turns a statistically significant lift into a decision not to launch.

Repo: https://github.com/yuvRaj244/credit-card-cashback-ab-test
